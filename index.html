The Cycle of Earth & Season

The Great Culinary Festivals of Europe

From the damp autumn oak forests yielding white truffles to ancient harvest grape-pressing and midsummer fish banquets under the midnight sun.

October – November

Alba White Truffle Fair

Piedmont, Italy

The world's most aromatic culinary spectacle. Truffle hunters (trifolao) and their trained dogs search misty Langhe hills for subterranean "white diamonds", paired with aged Barolo.

September – October

Rtveli & Tbilisoba Harvest

Kakheti & Tbilisi, Georgia

An 8,000-year-old unbroken ritual. Families crush indigenous Saperavi & Rkatsiteli grapes into clay Kvevri vessels, dipping walnut strings into hot grape Tatara for Churchkhela.

Late September

Fête de la Gastronomie

Paris & Burgundy, France

A nationwide celebration of France's UNESCO-honored food heritage. Open-air banquets, master chef demos, artisanal charcuterie tastings, and Grand Cru cellar tastings.

June Summer Solstice

Nordic Midsommar Feast

Stockholm & Dalarna, Sweden

Dancing under 24-hour daylight. Long rustic outdoor tables laden with pickled Baltic herring (sill), fresh new potatoes boiled with fresh dill, sour cream, and wild strawberries.
